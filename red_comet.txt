COMIC SCRIPT: Red Comet of the Belt

PAGE ONE (6 PANELS)

Panel 1
A sleek, crimson Soviet starfighter weaves through a dense field of asteroids in deep space.
CAPTION: 23rd Century. The Outer Belt.
CAPTION (NARRATION): Alexei Volkov — callsign "Red Comet" — patrols alone.

Panel 2
Close-up of Volkov in the cockpit, eyes scanning his instruments.
VOLKOV (Thought Bubble): Too quiet out here. Something's wrong.

Panel 3
Volkov's radar screen lights up with multiple contacts. He grips the controls.
VOLKOV: Чёрт! Damn — six bogeys closing fast!

Panel 4
Wide shot of enemy fighters emerging from behind an asteroid, weapons charging.

Panel 5
Volkov banks hard, his starfighter rolling through a narrow gap between asteroids.
VOLKOV: Come on, old girl. Don't fail me now.

Panel 6
Volkov fires his weapons. A massive explosion erupts as one enemy fighter is destroyed.
SFX: KRA-KOOM!
CAPTION: The Red Comet strikes first.

PAGE TWO (6 PANELS)

Panel 1
Volkov's fighter races through the asteroid field at high speed, enemies in pursuit.
SFX: VROOOOM!
VOLKOV (Thought Bubble): Three behind me. Two flanking. One... where's the sixth?

Panel 2
An enemy fighter appears directly ahead, weapons locked.
VOLKOV: There you are.

Panel 3
Volkov pulls a daring maneuver, diving under the oncoming fighter.
SFX: WHOOSH!

Panel 4
Two enemy fighters collide trying to follow Volkov's path through the asteroids.
SFX: KRAKK!
CAPTION (NARRATION): Volkov doesn't need fancy weapons. He lets the belt do the work.

Panel 5
Volkov loops behind the remaining fighters, lining up his shot.
VOLKOV: For the Motherland.

Panel 6
The final enemy fighter explodes in a brilliant flash. Volkov's crimson fighter streaks away.
SFX: KABOOM!
CAPTION: The Belt belongs to the Red Comet. For now.
